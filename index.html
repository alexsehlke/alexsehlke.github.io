Lone Star

Every star has a place.

A calm, clever logic puzzle for iPhone. Place the stars, light up the night sky, and collect a sticker for every one of 87 real constellations.

01 · Rules

One star in every row, column and region.

And no two stars may touch, not even diagonally. That’s all the rules. Everything else is logic: no guessing, no maths, no luck.

02 · Play

Tap, mark, deduce.

Tap once for a star and twice for an X. Long-press and drag to mark a whole row. Guide dots show what your stars already rule out, and a conflict lights up the moment it happens.

03 · Daily

Three new puzzles every day.

A Beginner 6×6, a Pro 8×8 and an Expert 10×10, the same for every player. Keep your streak going and see where your time ranks today.

04 · Sky map

Every solve lights a star.

Each star on the sky map is a puzzle. Solve every star in a constellation and its lines light up: Orion, Cassiopeia, Andromeda and 84 more.

05 · Stickers

A sticker for every constellation.

Finish a constellation and its hand-drawn sticker lands in your Sticker Book. 87 to collect, from Andromeda to Vulpecula.

06 · Badges

45 badges to earn.

Streaks, flawless solves, speed, X-marks, all five grid sizes. Some take a day, some take a year.

07 · Challenge

Race an AI to the last star.

In Challenge Mode you and an opponent solve the same grid at the same time. Its board fills in beside yours, mistakes and all. Choose Stargazer, Navigator or Supernova.

Unlocks after your first 10 puzzles.

08 · Battle Rush

Then try Battle Rush.

Five grids back to back, from 6×6 to 10×10. You and the AI race through all five, and whoever finishes the last grid first wins.

09 · Rankings

Climb the leaderboards.

Rankings for puzzles solved, time played, perfect solves, streaks, challenge wins and more. Today, this week or all time, worldwide or just your friends.

10 · Puzzles

12,500 puzzles in five sizes.

Beginner 6×6, Easy 7×7, Medium 8×8, Hard 9×9 and Expert 10×10, with 2,500 each. Finish a Beginner grid on a coffee break, or settle in with an Expert board. Every one plays offline.

Questions?

How do I play?

Place stars so that every row, every column and every outlined region holds exactly one star. No two stars may touch, not even diagonally. Tap a cell once for a star, again for an X, and a third time to clear it.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. All 12,500 puzzles are on your device. The free version uses the connection to show ads; leaderboards update when you're back online.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Your purchase is checked with the App Store and restored automatically.

I had Pro+. What now?

Pro+ is no longer sold. If you still subscribe, everything in Pro stays unlocked; cancel any time in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

How do I reset my progress?

In the app, go to Settings and tap Reset All Progress. This clears your completed puzzles, statistics and streaks, and can't be undone.

I found a bug or have an idea

Email alexsehlke@icloud.com. Your device model and iOS version help me track bugs down faster. You can also share puzzles and tips with other players in the Lone Star community on Reddit.
