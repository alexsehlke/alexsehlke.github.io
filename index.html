Lone Star

Somewhere in the night sky, the stars have scattered. Each one sits alone in its region, waiting to be found. Your mission: reunite them all.

A beautifully crafted star-placement puzzle game inspired by the classic Star Battle logic genre. Place stars on the grid so that every row, column, and region contains exactly the right number — but no two stars may touch, not even diagonally. Simple to learn, deeply satisfying to master.
